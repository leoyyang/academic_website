Our paper **[Interpretable Discriminative Text Representations via Agreement and
Label Disentanglement](/publication/interpretable_text/)** has been accepted for
presentation at the **14th Annual Meeting of Asian Political Methodology
(Asian PolMeth 2027)**, held at **The University of Hong Kong on January 8–9, 2027**.

I will present this joint work with **Tong Wang and Yiqing Xu** in a **panel session**.
The paper introduces **LLM-assisted Feature Discovery (LFD)**, a method for
identifying predictive text features with clear, reproducible definitions that
do not simply restate the prediction target.
