Leo Y. Yang

Research Assistant Professor
School of Business, Hong Kong Baptist University

The political economy of digital transformation.

My research examines how AI and digital technologies reshape work, and how information shapes political behavior and accountability, with a focus on China. I also develop AI methods for analyzing large-scale data and build tools that make empirical research more transparent and reproducible.

Background

I received my Ph.D. in Political Science, with a specialization in Computational Social Science, from UC San Diego in 2023. Before that, I earned an M.Phil. in Social Science from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (2016), an M.A. in Finance from the Wang Yanan Institute for Studies in Economics (WISE) at Xiamen University (2014), and a B.A. in Economics from Tan Kah Kee College, Xiamen University (2010).

I was previously a postdoctoral scholar at the Stanford Center on China’s Economy and Institutions (SCCEI). I am a faculty affiliate of Stanford SCCEI and the UC Institute on Global Conflict and Cooperation (IGCC).
